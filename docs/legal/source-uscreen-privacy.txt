# Privacy Policy - Albunyaan

Stichting Tarbiyah Consultancy operates this service and states: "We do not
rent, sell, or share your personal information with 3rd parties, except as
listed below."

Registration Requirements: The policy indicates users must provide "Full
Name, Address, Email Address, Phone, Country, State, Zip Code, and Billing
information" to access services.

Data Retention: Personal data is retained "for up to 30 days after your
account is deleted. After 30 days all your data is fully removed from our
system."

Analytics & Cookies: The service collects "cookies, IP Addresses, browser
type, device type, location, Internet service provider (ISP)" and similar
information to improve services.

Communications: Users automatically receive emails including "account
information, and other marketing related information" upon registration,
though they can opt out.

Security Measures: The policy notes data protection through "SSL encryption
and other security measures," though it acknowledges limitations: "we cannot
guarantee that our security measures will prevent unauthorized access."

Contact for Concerns: Users can reach the organization at
support@albunyaan.tv or info@albunyaan.tv regarding policy questions.

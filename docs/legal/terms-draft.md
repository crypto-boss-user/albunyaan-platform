- **Who we are:** Albunyaan.tv is operated by Stichting alAsr, a foundation

  already paid for — no fees, no need to call anyone.
- **14-day right of withdrawal:** under EU consumer law you normally have 14
  days to cancel a distance contract for free. Because this is digital
  content, we ask for your explicit consent at checkout to start delivering
  the service immediately — which means you give up that 14-day right once
  playback/access has started.
- **Vouchers:** non-transferable, cannot be exchanged for cash, and expire
  on the date shown on the voucher.
- **Fair use:** personal, household use only — no redistribution, scraping,
  or reverse engineering.
- **Liability:** the service is provided "as is"; our liability is capped
  at what you paid us in the past 12 months, except where Dutch law doesn't
  allow that cap (e.g. intentional harm).
- **Governing law:** the Netherlands, without prejudice to your rights as
  an EU consumer to bring a claim in your own country of residence.
